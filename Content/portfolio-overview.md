# University of Impact

## Elevator Pitch

College was designed for predictable careers: teach repeatable work, award a credential, then rely on employers to turn graduates into professionals through junior roles. AI is breaking both ends of that bargain. Repeatable work is becoming abundant, and the entry-level tasks that developed judgment are disappearing.

The University of Impact moves that development inside college. Students begin with a purpose, build AI fluency and professional depth, then spend two years in multidisciplinary capstone companies. Their personal agent force learns the same four-year corpus and mission context. Graduates can direct and challenge a system they have built.

Some capstone companies reach the market. Most fail. Either way, graduates leave with experience, visible capability, and an agent force no traditional program produces. Employers will compete for them. They will transform the companies they join or build the companies that come next.

## Image Concept

Anya stands inside a working greenhouse laboratory as a multidisciplinary student team builds and tests the systems around her. Plants, robotics, engineering tools, and an unfinished prototype make it clear that this is real work, not a classroom exercise. A subtle lunar landscape or habitat visualization hints at the larger ambition.

The image should feel optimistic, human, and achievable. Avoid graduation caps, lecture halls, floating AI brains, text, and generic holographic interfaces.
