## Crisis

### The PROBLEM

Education costs are rising while outcomes collapse

These loans prevent home purchases, business creation, family formation — slowing economic growth. Schools prepare students for disappearing jobs while banning AI. Degrees signal less while underemployment persists.

> "The world's biggest problems are the world's biggest business opportunities"
>
> — Peter Diamandis

1. Collapsing Trust: Americans' college value perception dropped 75% → 35% (15 years)
2. Cost Crisis: Tuition inflation 893% since 1983, far outpacing wages
3. Employment Failure: College graduates unemployed longest among all cohorts
4. Debt Catastrophe: 43M Americans carry $1.6T in student debt
