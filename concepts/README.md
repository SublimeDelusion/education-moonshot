# Concept Vault

**To make education more human.**

Education has traditionally optimized and tested for the possession of knowledge. This model changes the goal to the ability to put knowledge to work.

Students develop purpose, professional depth, intuition, judgment, and human relationships. Their agent forces and mission context extend the knowledge they can command. The institution creates the experiences through which that capability becomes real and visible.

## The central change

[Rethinking College in an Age of Abundant Intelligence](assumptions-worth-reexamining.md) develops the case for change through traditional education, what changed, and the opportunity: six practices to retire, five functions to redesign, and five human capabilities and experiences to elevate. It is a companion argument, not an additional core concept.

Personal tutoring is useful, but faster delivery of the old corpus is too small a goal. The student should leave with greater control over knowledge than they could achieve through memorization alone.

The human develops depth and judgment. The agent force supports breadth, retrieval, basic application, and continuity. The context library preserves how that knowledge relates to the mission. Their combined use develops capability.

Assessment should reveal whether the student can direct, connect, question, interpret, and act with that knowledge. The student's working system supports a human evaluation of the person.

## Distinct questions within the model

Consolidation keeps the important distinctions:

- **Purpose:** What is worth pursuing?
- **Capability:** What can the student command through personal understanding and their working system?
- **Standards:** What meaningful work should they be able to accomplish?
- **Assessment:** How do faculty establish the student's command and judgment?
- **Professional proof:** How can someone outside the institution inspect and test that capability?
- **Human formation:** What relationships and experiences develop the person carrying the responsibility?

Standards and assessment are distinct responsibilities within Demonstrated Mastery. Tutoring, AI use, and the graduating agent force are mechanisms within Mastery Over Knowledge. Cross-teaching and contact with practicing experts are mechanisms within Education Becomes More Human.

Earlier materials sometimes describe AI primarily as a way to accelerate transfer of a conventional professional corpus. The current concept changes that objective: build human depth and intuition together with command of a wider corpus through agents and context. This should guide the next operating-model and artifact revisions.

Older material also retains degrees and accreditation as sources of value, and some assessment passages suggest unaided checks. Those remain visible in the sources for deliberate reconciliation. They should not override the current theses. Story outcomes illustrate the ideas rather than validate them.
