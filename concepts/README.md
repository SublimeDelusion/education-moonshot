# Concept Vault

**To make education more human.**

This vault develops the core intellectual propositions of the Education Moonshot. Each concept has its own thesis, reasoning, mechanisms, connections, and questions that still need work. These are the ideas the operating model exists to put into practice.

The concepts are organized by the assumptions they change and the capabilities they create. The [operating model](../model/operating-model.md) describes a current implementation. [Assets](../assets/README.md) explain it to particular audiences. [Strategies](../strategy/README.md) explore ways to establish it.

## The initial set

| Concept | Question | Core proposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Purpose Replaces the Degree Program](purpose-replaces-the-degree-program.md) | What organizes the student's education? | Purpose directs the path; professional specialization develops the depth. |
| [2. Personal Tutoring Without the Classroom Pace](personal-tutoring-without-the-classroom-pace.md) | Why must everyone learn at the pace of the room? | Continuous individual instruction removes a central constraint of the classroom. |
| [3. AI Is Part of the Education](ai-is-part-of-the-education.md) | Should students use AI? | Learning to direct AI is a core professional capability. |
| [4. Higher Capability, Higher Standards](higher-capability-higher-standards.md) | What should students now be expected to accomplish? | Available capability raises the ambition and substance of the work. |
| [5. Assessment Through Application and Defense](assessment-through-application-and-defense.md) | How do we know the student understands? | Apply, explain, defend, and adapt with AI available. |
| [6. Credentialing Is Dead. The Work Is the Proof.](the-work-is-the-proof.md) | What does the graduate need to prove capability? | A discoverable corpus, attributable contribution, live application, and credible references. |
| [7. Capstone Corporations Move the First Job Into College](capstone-corporations.md) | Where does professional experience come from? | Real organizations supply the responsibility the first career rung used to provide. |
| [8. Education Becomes More Human](education-becomes-more-human.md) | What is the institution for? | Human formation, relationships, judgment, and interdependence become central. |
| [9. The Living Library of Applications](living-library-of-applications.md) | How does personalization enrich shared learning? | Each student's purpose creates another application the cohort can learn from. |
| [10. Education Must Survive Impact](education-must-survive-impact.md) | When should the work encounter the outside world? | From the beginning, with increasing consequence. |
| [11. The Student's Agent Force Graduates With Them](the-students-agent-force.md) | What durable professional system does the student build? | Curated context and an agent force that knows the work and the person. |
| [12. The Institution Compounds](the-institution-compounds.md) | What does each cohort leave for the next? | Experience, infrastructure, relationships, and potential capital accumulate. |

Read the first six as an argument: release the degree-program constraint, personalize instruction, teach with AI, raise the standard, demonstrate understanding, and make capability directly inspectable. The remaining concepts describe the human and institutional environment that makes those commitments possible.

## Distinctions that matter

| Concept | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| AI use | Which capabilities students learn to direct. |
| Learning standards | What students should be capable of accomplishing. |
| Assessment | How faculty establish that a particular student understands. |
| Professional proof | How another person can inspect and test the graduate's capability. |

Purpose is also distinct from professional specialty. The student begins with a purpose and develops serious disciplinary depth. Removing the conventional degree as the organizing container does not remove expertise.

The capstone corporation joins the missing-first-rung thesis to its implementation mechanism. Human development is the central purpose running through pods, cross-teaching, salon culture, recruiting, networks, and consequential teams.

## How to maintain the vault

Develop an idea here until its meaning and implications are clear. Keep reasoning and unresolved questions with the concept. When a mechanism becomes part of the operating model, link the concept to that implementation. When a concept is superseded or retired, move it to the same path beneath `archive/` and explain the change in the archive index.

There is no separate decisions folder. The vault should preserve the strongest articulation of an idea and make changes in its meaning traceable.

[Open design questions](open-design-questions.md) collects cross-cutting mechanics that still need resolution.

## Changes to reconcile in other artifacts

The vault now states the stronger credentialing thesis: the work establishes capability directly. The older business case still includes a recognized degree, tuition for an accredited program, and accreditation as a prerequisite in parts of its implementation logic. The story depicts degrees at graduation. Those passages remain in their source documents for a deliberate later edit; they should not be read back into the concept as a requirement that credentials supply the proof.

The business case also mentions selected unaided checks. The current assessment concept centers application and defense with AI available, as specified in the reviewed operating model and Jason's latest direction.

Earlier pod descriptions vary in size, and the story supplies specific cohort and company counts. This vault preserves the mechanisms without turning illustrative numbers into fixed institutional requirements.
