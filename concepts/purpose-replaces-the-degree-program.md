# Purpose Replaces the Degree Program

**The student no longer needs to choose a degree program as the organizing container for their education. They need a purpose worth pursuing and a path to becoming capable of pursuing it.**

## The constraint we can release

The conventional starting question asks an inexperienced student to select a major before they have encountered much of the work that profession actually does. That choice then supplies a predetermined sequence of subjects, assignments, and requirements. The student spends years inside the container and eventually discovers how much of it connects to the work they care about.

The Education Moonshot begins with a different question: what do you care enough about to pursue?

A purpose can cross several disciplines. It can persist while the student's understanding of the problem, preferred profession, and eventual role all change. Choosing a degree is no longer necessary to give education structure when the institution can help construct and maintain a learning path around that purpose.

This is an institutional design choice. It changes what organizes the education, who helps navigate it, and why the student seeks knowledge.

## Purpose creates the demand for learning

The existing concept expresses the learning loop as:

**purpose → problem → knowledge needed → learning → application → feedback → deeper problem**

The student encounters something they cannot yet understand or accomplish. That creates a reason to learn. Applying the new knowledge reveals the next gap. Sometimes the next step is deeper personal understanding; sometimes it is retrieving, connecting, or directing knowledge through the student's agent force. The goal is command of what the work requires.

Purpose therefore does more than improve motivation. It changes the relationship between knowledge and work. The student can explain why an idea matters to something they are trying to do, and the result of using it supplies feedback on their understanding.

A meaningful purpose does not require a fully formed business, a fixed career, or a promise to solve a global problem. In the story, the entrance advisor helps Anya articulate what connects the problems she cares about. Her purpose becomes a direction broad enough to survive changes in her project.

## Professional depth still matters

The removal of the degree program as the four-year container does not remove disciplines, prerequisites, or expertise.

The current operating model begins with breadth. Students explore fields, meet practitioners, use departmental resources, and learn enough to make an informed choice about where to develop depth. At the end of Year One, they declare a professional specialty.

Year Two is deliberately structured. The department identifies the professional knowledge, methods, and learning sequence. The student develops deep understanding where the work demands it, while building the agent force and context library through which a much broader corpus can be used. The student's purpose guides applications within and across disciplines. Years Three and Four deepen intuition and judgment through responsibility and increasingly consequential work.

The distinction is fundamental:

- **Purpose** gives the student a reason and direction.
- **Specialty** gives them expertise other people can depend on.
- **A project or company** provides a particular setting in which to apply that expertise.

These can change on different timelines. A graduate can leave a company without losing their profession or surrendering their purpose.

## Structure follows the learner

An advisor works with the student to turn purpose into an exploration plan. Subject faculty identify introductory corpora and prerequisite gaps. Departmental placements expose the student to methods and resources they would not know to seek alone. The plan changes as the student learns.

This is supported self-direction. The institution remains responsible for access, challenge, coherence, and standards. The student remains responsible for effort, choices, and work.

The four-year progression is one implementation of the concept. Its deeper commitment is that a student's ambition should not have to fit inside a preselected degree before it can become an education.

## Questions to develop

How does the institution help a student who has only a broad interest? How are changes of purpose handled once substantial specialization has begun? What common foundations should every student encounter, including knowledge their current purpose would not lead them to request? The model establishes the advisor, exploration year, and common core; detailed admissions and progression mechanics remain open.
