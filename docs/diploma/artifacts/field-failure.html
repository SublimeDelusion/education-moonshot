Decision and failure record

Recovering from a field power failure

Overview

Investigated an intermittent reset during a rover field trial.

Authorship and contribution

Alex coordinated reproduction, compared motor-current traces with reset events, and worked with Jordan Lee on cable routing and connector strain relief.

Method and reasoning

Returned to controlled fault reproduction before changing the entire board. Recorded both the electrical hypothesis and the mechanical contributors.

Outcomes

The team revised protection settings and the harness, then added the failure condition to regression testing.

Scope and limitations

The record demonstrates a diagnostic approach; it does not establish universal reliability.
