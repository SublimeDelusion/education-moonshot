Inspection rover power system

Overview

Designed and integrated the rover's protected power-distribution board.

Authorship and contribution

Alex authored the board schematic, selected protection components, and wrote firmware hooks for voltage and current telemetry. Mechanical packaging and the rover's navigation software were teammates' work.

Method and reasoning

Separated motor and compute power paths so a motor transient would not repeatedly reset the onboard computer.

Outcomes

The bench record includes schematic revisions, a fault-injection checklist, and telemetry traces showing how the protected paths behaved.

Scope and limitations

This record has no independently verified runtime, deployment count, or certification result.
