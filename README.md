# Education Moonshot

**To make education more human.**

A model for purpose-driven, AI-accelerated post-secondary education centered on the student and the human connections they need to succeed.

University of Impact is the working institutional model developed here.

## Source ownership and publishing

This repository owns the education model and the canonical manuscript of *Anya's Ascent*. The story belongs with the model because it explores the student experience and helps explain and test the institution's design.

[Hopeful Dystopia](https://github.com/SublimeDelusion/hopeful-dystopai) presents a published reader edition. Its existing manuscript snapshot, website, and audio experience remain publication assets, not a competing source of truth. Published editions may lag the working manuscript; revisions should originate here and be deliberately incorporated into reader releases.

The operating model is still under development. Its September 27 handoff identifies the reviewed Year One, Year Two, and Summer Founders Program sections; later sections remain working drafts. Fictional outcomes illustrate the model and are not evidence that it has been validated.
